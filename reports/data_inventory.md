- `meter_reading` units are not explicitly stated in the downloadable CSV. The competition documentation defines the target as energy consumed in kWh; the re-uploaded file does not add a unit column. Units are therefore reported as **dataset energy units (documented as kWh)** and are not independently re-verified from the file.
- Repeated timestamps across different buildings are expected and are not duplicates. The duplicate check uses the business key.
- Zero readings are retained in the audit; their treatment is decided in the cleaning stage and documented in the report.
